Who We Are

Heart of the Islamic Community

Al Bukhary Mosque is more than just a place of worship; it is a thriving hub for the community. Established on the core values of peace, compassion, and unity, we provide a welcoming space for everyone to learn, pray, and grow together.

We offer comprehensive educational programs, charitable initiatives, and community services aimed at uplifting individuals and families in accordance with Islamic teachings.

  • Welcoming environment for all
  • Authentic Islamic Education
  • Dedicated Community Support

What We Offer

Our Services

Islamic Education

Comprehensive Madrasa for children and adults, focusing on Quranic memorization, Tajweed, and Islamic studies.

Zakat & Charity

Collection and distribution of Zakat, Sadaqah, and running food banks for the less fortunate in our community.

Marriage (Nikah)

Official Islamic marriage ceremonies, pre-marital counseling, and family dispute resolution services.

Funeral Services

Complete Janazah services including washing (Ghusl), shrouding, funeral prayer, and burial arrangements.
